# Adding spending

## Type or say it

Write a payment the way you would say it. Examples:

| You write | Budgie records |
| --- | --- |
| `Uber 23.40 a week ago` | Uber, −$23.40, Transport, dated a week back |
| `spent 12.50 on lunch at Chipotle last friday` | Chipotle, −$12.50, Food & dining, note "Lunch" |
| `got paid 2350 salary on the 1st` | Salary, +$2,350, Income |
| `paid 1.2k rent last month` | Rent, −$1,200, Housing |
| `refund 40 from amazon today` | Amazon, +$40, note "Refund" |

Dates it understands: today, yesterday, the day before yesterday, N days, weeks, months or years ago, in N days or weeks, last or next week, month or year, last or next weekday, on the 3rd, and calendar dates.

Click the microphone to dictate. Words appear as you speak; the entry is added when you stop. Dictation works in Chrome, Edge and Safari.

## Scan or upload

- **Scan receipt:** take a photo; text is read in your browser.
- **Upload:** receipt photos, PDFs and invoices.
- **Import statement:** CSV or Excel files from your bank. Budgie finds the date, description and amount columns.

## Review

Everything waits in the review queue. Change the company (tap the logo), amount, currency, category or date, then accept one item or all.
