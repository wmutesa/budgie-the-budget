# Features

## Overview
A written brief of your month, net worth with 1M, 3M and 1Y ranges, a monthly plan, cash flow, savings goals, live exchange rates, subscriptions, expense breakdown, accounts and wallets converted to your base currency.

## Search and navigation
**Voice from anywhere:** tap the microphone in the top bar and say one or more things ("essay due Friday at 5, then spent 9 on coffee yesterday"). Budgie splits what you said and sends each thing to the Planner, Transactions or your money plan, then shows what went where with Undo.

The search in the top bar finds tasks, spending, subscriptions, goals, accounts, calendars and pages, and turns typed text into "add to Planner" or "add spending". Press `/` to jump to it.

## Planner
One place for school, work, projects, events and daily life.
- **Add from the Playground inbox, the search bar or a calendar slot.** "Essay due Fri 5pm #English !", "Chem lecture MWF 10-12", "Gym 3x a week at 6pm", "Pay rent on the 1st $1450". Budgie reads the date, time, length, repeat, project (`#name`), priority (`!`) and cost (`$`), and shows what it understood before you add it.
- **Four views.** Tasks (grouped by when), Agenda (the next two weeks), Week (seven columns) and Focus (progress today, what is on now, your top three and a 25-minute focus timer).
- **Calendars.** Paste the iCal link from Canvas, Google Calendar, Apple iCloud or Outlook. Canvas items become assignments under their course; repeating classes stay repeating. Calendars refresh every few hours, and your ticks and notes are kept.
- **Routine.** Give your wake and sleep times and a few words about your week; Budgie drafts a daily routine (the on-device AI refines it when ready) and you choose what to keep. Repeating items keep a streak.
- **Suggestions.** Work sessions before deadlines, daily review before exams, decisions before renewals, money set aside for goals, and gaps in your routine. Ask the on-device AI for more ideas.
- **Linked to money.** Tick something with a cost and it waits in Transactions to review. The bell lists what is due today, what is overdue and deadlines tomorrow. Optional reminders on this device ten minutes before timed items, while Budgie is open.

## Transactions
Four summary tiles, the Add spending panel, a review queue, monthly budgets and recent activity. Budgets update as soon as you accept new items.

## Reports
- **What Budgie thinks:** a short read on your month from an AI model on your own device (in the browser or Ollama), or from built-in rules.
- **Action cards:** suggestions you can apply in one tap, such as raising a budget that is always exceeded.
- **Savings and investing:** ideas for Chase and Bank of America based on your numbers, linking to each bank's own rate page.
- **Year in numbers** and **All activity** side by side.

## Playground
**Inbox:** write or say anything, one thing per line. Budgie sends things to do to the Planner, money already spent to Transactions, and amounts that repeat to your money plan, and you can change each line's destinations before sending.

Write or say messy notes. Budgie sorts them into needs, wants and savings, converts yearly, weekly and daily costs to monthly, compares the plan with common splits, and can turn the plan into your monthly budgets.

## Subscriptions
Search any service by name; Budgie finds its website and logo. Long lists show six at a time with a "show more" toggle.

## Currencies
160+ currencies through live rates, including RWF and THB. Pick a base currency on the Overview.
