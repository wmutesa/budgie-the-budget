# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want Taskie to be a welcoming project for everyone, whatever their background, identity or experience. We pledge to make taking part a harassment-free experience.

## What we expect

- Be respectful and considerate in issues, pull requests, reviews and discussions.
- Give and receive feedback about the work, not the person.
- Assume good intent, and ask questions when something is unclear.
- Help newcomers find their way.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or derogatory comments.
- Discriminatory language or imagery.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussions.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, commits, issues and other contributions that break this code, and may ban contributors who do so repeatedly.

To report a problem, contact the maintainer privately through GitHub ([@wmutesa](https://github.com/wmutesa)). Reports are handled confidentially.
